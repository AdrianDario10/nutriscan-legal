Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

NutriScan AI (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the NutriScan AI mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

NutriScan AI does not require user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or phone number.

The App may process the following data:

  • Barcode data scanned by the user to retrieve food product information.
  • User preferences (language, tone, dietary profile), stored locally on the device.
  • Usage data (daily scan and chat counts), stored locally on the device.

This information is used solely to provide the core functionality of the App.

2. Local Storage

All preferences, usage counters, and scan history are stored locally on your device using secure local storage. NutriScan AI does not create user profiles on external servers.

3. AI Processing

When scanning a product or asking a question, the App sends the barcode data and relevant product information to a secure backend server to generate an AI-based response.

This processing is necessary to analyze food composition and provide informational summaries to the user.

No personal identifiers are included in these requests.

AI-generated responses are provided for informational purposes only and do not constitute medical or professional advice.

4. Advertising

NutriScan AI displays advertisements in the free version of the App using Google AdMob.

Advertising data may include device identifiers used for ad delivery and frequency capping.

AdMob may collect device information such as advertising identifiers to show relevant ads. This data is handled by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users may manage ad personalization preferences through their device settings.

5. In-App Purchases

NutriScan AI offers optional in-app subscriptions to unlock PRO features such as increased usage limits and an ad-free experience. All payments are processed securely by Apple via the App Store. NutriScan AI does not have access to users’ payment information.

6. Analytics

NutriScan AI does not use third-party analytics tools to track individual users.

7. Data Sharing

NutriScan AI does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Any data processed by third-party services (such as advertising providers) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

NutriScan AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. User Rights

Since NutriScan AI does not collect or store personal data on its servers, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete externally. All locally stored data can be removed by uninstalling the App.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the App and its backend services. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Health Disclaimer

NutriScan AI provides nutritional and food-related information for general informational purposes only.

The App does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or personalized health advice.

Users should consult qualified health professionals for medical or dietary decisions.
