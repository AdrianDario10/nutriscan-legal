<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p><strong>Last updated:</strong> January 2026</p>

<p>
NutriScan (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it.
This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the NutriScan mobile application.
</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
<p>
NutriScan does <strong>not require user accounts</strong> and does <strong>not collect personally identifiable information</strong> such as name, email address, or phone number.
</p>

<p>The App may process the following data:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Barcode data</strong> scanned by the user to retrieve food product information.</li>
  <li><strong>User preferences</strong> (language, tone, dietary profile), stored locally on the device.</li>
  <li><strong>Usage data</strong> (daily scan and chat counts), stored locally on the device.</li>
</ul>

<p>
This information is used solely to provide the core functionality of the App.
</p>

<h2>2. Local Storage</h2>
<p>
All preferences, usage counters, and scan history are stored locally on your device using secure local storage.
NutriScan does not create user profiles on external servers.
</p>

<h2>3. AI Processing</h2>
<p>
When scanning a product or asking a question, the App sends the barcode data and relevant product information to a secure backend server to generate an AI-based response.
</p>

<p>
No personal identifiers are included in these requests.
</p>

<h2>4. Advertising</h2>
<p>
NutriScan displays advertisements in the free version of the App using Google AdMob.
</p>

<p>
AdMob may collect device information such as advertising identifiers to show relevant ads.
This data is handled by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy:
</p>

<p>
<a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
https://policies.google.com/privacy
</a>
</p>

<h2>5. Analytics</h2>
<p>
NutriScan does not use third-party analytics tools to track individual users.
</p>

<h2>6. Data Sharing</h2>
<p>
NutriScan does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.
Any data processed by third-party services (such as advertising providers) is governed by their respective privacy policies.
</p>

<h2>7. Children’s Privacy</h2>
<p>
NutriScan is not intended for children under the age of 13.
The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
</p>

<h2>8. User Rights</h2>
<p>
Since NutriScan does not collect or store personal data on its servers, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete externally.
All locally stored data can be removed by uninstalling the App.
</p>

<h2>9. Security</h2>
<p>
Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the App and its backend services.
However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.
</p>

<h2>10. Changes to This Policy</h2>
<p>
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
</p>
